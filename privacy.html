Privacy Policy

Nakat: Car Expenses & Service · Effective October 8, 2026

Nakat is an app for tracking car expenses, fuel-ups, service, and repairs. The developer is Kiryl Sytskou ("we"). Contact: kirill.sytkov.dev@gmail.com.

In short

  • Your entries, vehicles, documents, and photos stay on your iPhone and in your personal iCloud. We have no server that receives them, and we never see them.
  • The app has no ads, no tracking across apps and websites, and does not sell data.
  • To improve the app and find crashes, we collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase. You can turn this off in Settings → Analytics and Crash Reports.

What stays on your device and in iCloud

Vehicles, fuel-ups, service, repairs, expenses, income, odometer readings, reminders, documents (numbers and dates), and photos of receipts and work orders. The data is stored in the app on your device and synced through the private iCloud database of your Apple Account. We have no access to it. If iCloud is off, the data stays on the device only.

What analytics collect

For analytics and crash reports we use Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics). The data processor is Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC; we have accepted Google's data processing terms.

Collected:

  • App usage — which screens were opened and which actions were taken, for example "fuel-up added" or "paywall opened". Only action types and coarse ranges (for example "2 to 3 vehicles", "entry took under 5 seconds").
  • Purchases — the Nakat PRO plan chosen and the outcome (success, cancelled, failed). We do not send amounts or prices.
  • Device and app information — device model, iOS version, app version, language, units and currency, whether notifications are allowed, whether Pro is active.
  • Approximate location — country and region are derived from the IP address; the app does not request or send precise location.
  • Firebase app instance ID — a random installation identifier. It is not linked to your name, Apple Account, advertising identifier, or phone number.
  • Crash reports — technical information about what the app was doing when it crashed: call stack, device and app state.

Not collected: amounts, prices, odometer values, vehicle or gas station names, VINs, document numbers, notes, names of services and categories, photos, contacts, precise location. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used, the App Tracking Transparency prompt is not shown, and the data is not linked to you or used for advertising. Google Signals and ads personalization are turned off.

Why

To understand which features are used, where people run into trouble, and to fix bugs and improve stability. The data is not sold or shared with anyone other than Google as our processor.

How long we keep it

Google Analytics events and properties — 14 months, then deleted automatically. Crashlytics crash reports — 90 days.

International transfers

Google processes data including on servers in the United States. Transfers rely on Google's data processing terms and standard contractual clauses where required.

How to turn it off

Settings → Analytics and Crash Reports is a switch. When it is off, the app stops collecting statistics and sending crash reports, and any unsent reports are deleted. You can turn it back on at any time. The app works the same with analytics on or off.

Your rights

Analytics data cannot identify you, so we usually cannot find and delete "your" events on request. If you want to exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, or objection, or you have questions, write to kirill.sytkov.dev@gmail.com. Turning the switch off stops further collection. You may also contact the data protection authority in your country.

Purchases and feedback

Payments for Nakat PRO are processed by Apple (App Store); we do not receive your payment details. If you write to us from the app, the email is sent from your mail app; diagnostics include the app version, iOS, device model, and the number of vehicles and entries — never the content of your entries.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date and publish the new version on this page. We will tell you about significant changes in the app.

Nasıl kapatılır

Ayarlar → Analiz ve çökme raporları bir anahtardır. Kapalı olduğunda uygulama istatistik toplamayı ve çökme raporu göndermeyi durdurur, gönderilmemiş raporlar silinir. İstediğiniz zaman yeniden açabilirsiniz. Uygulama, analiz açık da olsa kapalı da olsa aynı şekilde çalışır.
