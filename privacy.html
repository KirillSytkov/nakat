Privacy Policy

Nakat: Car Expenses & Service · Effective October 4, 2026

Short version: Nakat does not collect any data. We don't have servers, accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your data belongs to you.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter — cars, fill-ups, services, expenses, reminders, documents and photos — is stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is enabled, it is synced through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). Only you can access it; the developer cannot see, read or restore it.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up or account.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data.

Notifications

Reminders about maintenance, documents and mileage are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

Nakat PRO subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. The app only receives the status of your purchase from the App Store; we never receive your payment details.

Export and sharing

CSV exports and PDF reports are created on your device and shared only where you choose to send them.

Contacting support

If you write to us, we receive your email address and the message you send. If you choose to include diagnostics, they contain only the app version, iOS version, device model and the number of cars and records — never the content of your records. We use this only to answer you.

Deleting your data

Delete entries or cars in the app, or delete the app. To remove synced data, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage on your iPhone.

Children

Nakat is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Política de privacidad

Nakat: Gastos y mantenimiento · vigente desde el 4 de octubre de 2026

En resumen: Nakat no recopila ningún dato. No tenemos servidores, cuentas, anuncios, analítica ni rastreo. Tus datos son tuyos.

Dónde se guardan tus datos

Todo lo que introduces —coches, repostajes, revisiones, gastos, recordatorios, documentos y fotos— se guarda en tu iPhone. Si iCloud está activado, se sincroniza mediante tu base de datos privada de iCloud (Apple CloudKit). Solo tú tienes acceso; el desarrollador no puede verlos, leerlos ni restaurarlos.

Lo que no hacemos

  • Sin registro ni cuenta.
  • Sin publicidad, analítica, informes de errores ni otros SDK de terceros.
  • Sin rastreo entre apps o sitios web, y sin venta ni cesión de datos.

Notificaciones

Los recordatorios de mantenimiento, documentos y kilometraje son notificaciones locales programadas en tu dispositivo. No se envía nada a ningún servidor.

Compras

Las suscripciones Nakat PRO y la compra «Para siempre» las gestiona Apple. La app solo recibe del App Store el estado de la compra; nunca recibimos tus datos de pago.

Exportación

Las exportaciones CSV y los informes PDF se crean en tu dispositivo y solo se comparten donde tú decidas.

Soporte

Si nos escribes, recibimos tu correo y tu mensaje. Si adjuntas diagnóstico, solo incluye la versión de la app, la versión de iOS, el modelo del dispositivo y el número de coches y registros, nunca su contenido. Lo usamos únicamente para responderte.
